# See who is working on what

- A new button in the top bar shows the faces of the crew members at work, with a red dot when one of them is waiting for your approval. Working agents grow little horns.
- It opens the work board: each agent's task, what it is doing this second (and which scene, shot or character), the stage it works in, its steps and what the run has cost so far, live.
- A task one agent hands another hangs under the agent that asked, so you can follow who gave whom what.
- Finished work stays on the board with its outcome, cost and closing words; tap a line to see what it changed.
- Scenes, shots, characters and places that an agent is working on carry its face, and the stages in the navigation show who is in them.
- Agents read the same board (`crew.work.list`), so the coordinator knows who is busy before it hands out work.
