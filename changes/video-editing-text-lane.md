# Text on the picture

- A text lane above the picture on the Edit screen: add titles, lower thirds (a name and, on the next line, who they are), captions pinned in the frame and subtitles at the playhead, then drag, lengthen and change them like any other piece.
- Subtitles in one click: every line that is heard gets subtitles, split at sentences, and they keep up when you move, trim or re-record the lines.
- End credits in one click: the title, the cast and the crew roll up over a black card at the end of the scene.
- Text styles for the whole film, with a live picture of each: font, size, colour, outline, shadow, box, place in the frame, and how a text comes on (fade, rise, typed) and goes off.
- What you see in the preview is what the exported film shows: texts are burned in, subtitles optionally, and the subtitles can also be downloaded as an .srt file.
- The Film Editor can do all of this too; its texts arrive as proposed changes you keep or reject.
- Fixed: changing one thing about a shot, a placed sound, a character, a place or an object no longer quietly resets other settings (such as the objects in a shot, or the pan of a sound).
