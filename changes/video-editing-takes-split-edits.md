# Takes and J/L cuts

- Making a clip again no longer throws the old one away. Each shot keeps its earlier clips as takes (up to 20), shown under the clip on the Shots stage. Tap one to watch it, and put it back with "Use".
- Dropping a video from the library onto a shot that already has a clip now keeps the old clip as a take.
- On the Edit stage, a shot's inspector has a take picker, so you can try an earlier take right in the cut. Trims are kept.
- New J and L cuts. Select a shot and choose "J cut" to hear the next shot before you see it, or "L cut" to let this shot's sound run on under the next picture. Imp moves the sound to the audio lane, keeps everything in sync, and the scene stays the same length.
- The Film Editor can do all of this too: it can list and swap takes and make J and L cuts with the same commands.
- Subtitles stay on their line when its sound is moved to the audio lane.
- Sound taken apart from a clip now ends where its picture ended, instead of playing on to the end of the clip.
