# An Imp coordinator for all projects

- The crew panel now has two chats, switched at its top: **All projects** (blue) and your open project (amber, with its name), so it is always clear which coordinator you are talking to.
- The **Imp coordinator** under All projects looks after what concerns every project: settings and models, model mixes, saved crews and roles, and creating, copying, opening or deleting projects. The start screen shows it.
- Each project's coordinator keeps to its project. Ask it for something that concerns all projects, such as switching the language, and it passes it on: its chat says **Passed to the Imp coordinator**, and the Imp coordinator's chat shows the ask with the project it came from.
- Ask the Imp coordinator for a film and it creates the project and hands the work to that project's coordinator.
