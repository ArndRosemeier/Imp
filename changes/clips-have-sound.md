# Clips have sound again

- Every new clip comes with sound, also shots where nobody speaks. Before, Imp asked the video model for silence on those, so most clips played nothing. A clip is only silent when you ask for that.
- Characters on screen speak their lines in the clip itself, so the voice matches their lips. A recorded voice is only laid over a clip for a narrator or a voice off screen, and the clip keeps its own sound under it.
- Video models that make no sound are marked unsuitable in the model picker and are not used for clips.
