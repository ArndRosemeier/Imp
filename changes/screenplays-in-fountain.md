# Real screenplays in the Script stage

- Scenes are now shown as a proper screenplay page: names centred, dialogue indented, directions in brackets, transitions on the right, in Courier as scripts are set. Switch between Page and Write above each scene.
- Scenes are written in Fountain, the plain-text format screenwriting apps use. In Write, Tab turns a line into the next part (action, character, direction, dialogue, transition), and buttons do the same on a tablet. The cast's names are one tap away.
- The Script header shows how long the film runs: pages, and about a minute a page.
- Each line of dialogue on the page shows which shot speaks it. In Shots, "From the script" fills a shot's line, speaker and delivery from the script, and shot lines that are not in the script are listed under the scene, ready to add.
- Download the script as a PDF in the standard screenplay layout (with a title page, page numbers and MORE/CONT'D at page breaks), as Fountain, or as Final Draft (.fdx).
- Import a Fountain or Final Draft script, and its scenes are added after yours.
- The Screenwriter and every other agent can do all of this through the same commands.
