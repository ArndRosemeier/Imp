# Transitions between shots

- A shot can now come in with a dissolve, a dip to black, a dip to white or a wipe instead of a straight cut. Pick it under "Comes in with" in the inspector and set how long it takes.
- A dissolve or wipe lays the shot over the end of the one before, so the scene gets a little shorter, as in any editor. A dip fades out and back in at the cut and keeps the length.
- The sound of the two shots crossfades during a dissolve or wipe.
- The Edit preview, the exported film and the browser recording all show the same transition. The timeline marks each one at the start of its shot.
- Agents set transitions with the same command (item.set), and see them in the edit decision list.
