# The whole film on the Edit screen

- The Edit screen now opens on a film strip: every scene is a block as long as the scene, green when all its pieces are ready, amber while some are missing, dashed when empty. Tap a scene to open its timeline below.
- Zoom the strip from the whole film down to one scene with Fit, plus and minus, a pinch, or Ctrl and the mouse wheel.
- A scene's timeline now zooms down to single frames, with a line at every frame. Comma and full stop step one frame back or on, and Fit shows the whole scene.
- Play on: when a scene ends, the next one opens and keeps playing, so you can watch the film through.
- Markers: leave a note, a to-do or a problem at the playhead with the Marker button or M. They sit on their own lane, follow the shot they were put on, and show on the film strip. Mark them done when dealt with.
- Agents can leave and read markers too, and read the whole film as one text, scene by scene.
