# Help: a guide, first-time tips, and one switch to turn them off

- A new Help button in the top bar opens the Imp guide: a page on getting started, the crew, costs, the library, projects, running Imp on your own computer, tablets and shortcuts, and one page for every stage. Search it by any word.
- The first time you open a stage, a short tip says what it is for and what to do first. "Got it" closes it; "Read more" opens its page in the guide.
- Know your way around? "Turn off all tips" removes every tip at once. The guide stays. Turn tips back on in the guide or in Settings, where "Show closed tips again" brings back the ones you closed.
- Agents can read the guide and turn tips on or off too, so you can ask the coordinator how something works.
