# Ask Imp

- The Help button now opens Ask Imp: ask anything about Imp in your own words and get an answer from its guide, with the stage and button to use.
- Ask it to do the step ("turn off all tips", "add a shot to scene 2") and it does it for you with the same commands you have. It asks before spending money or deleting anything.
- Example questions to start with, and the guide is one tab away.
- The coordinator now reads the guide too when you ask it how something works, and says what a step will cost before it spends.
