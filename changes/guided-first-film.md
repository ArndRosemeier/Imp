# Your first film, step by step

- A slim bar above each stage shows the six steps to a first film: the idea, a scene, a character, the shots, one clip and the cut. It shows which are done and what comes next.
- Steps tick themselves off from what the project holds, whether you did them by hand, through an agent or got them from the sample.
- "Go to" takes you to the right stage. "Ask the coordinator" puts the request in the coordinator's message box; nothing is sent until you press Send.
- Close it for good with the ×, or turn off all tips. Agents read the same steps with `project.path`.
