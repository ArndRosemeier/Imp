# Edit dialogue by its words

- The audio editor can write down what is said in each region, word by word with times (Words, then "Write down what is said"). It costs a fraction of a cent a minute.
- Tap a word, then another, to pick everything between: Delete removes the words and closes the gap, Silence leaves the pause, and Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V move them.
- Cuts fall in the pauses around words, so the speech either side sounds natural.
- The word being spoken lights up while the sound plays.
- Agents can transcribe, read and cut dialogue by its words too.
