# Imp so far

- Connect an OpenRouter key, pick a coordinator model, and the coordinator can set everything else up for you.
- Hire a crew of AI agents (Screenwriter, Casting Director, Clip Designer, Sound Designer, Film Editor, Sound Editor) or define your own roles, such as critics or advisors. Everything you can do in Imp, they can do too.
- Projects with a start screen, templates, duplicate, export and import, and copying characters, scenes and more between projects.
- Development and Script: write the idea, the scenes and the screenplay yourself or with the Screenwriter.
- Casting: characters with portraits and voice samples.
- Design: locations and objects with their views and states, so the world stays consistent from shot to shot.
- Shots: video clips per shot, starting on a key frame you choose, with dialogue spoken over them.
- Sound: effects and music per scene, and a full audio editor with lanes, region effects and loudness.
- Edit: a cut-and-paste film editor with picture and sound kept separate, titles, fades, pan, undo, and export of a film as a video.
- Works in the browser, or on your own computer with the optional local server, and on landscape tablets with touch.
