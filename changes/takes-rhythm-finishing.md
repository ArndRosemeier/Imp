# Key shots, a fine cut and a finished sound

- **Key shot: two takes** on a shot marks one the film rests on. The Clip Designer makes it twice and keeps the better take.
- After the first cuts, the Film Editor makes a rhythm pass over the whole film. The **fine cut** is a new, last checkpoint in Edit; the film's look and end credits wait for it.
- Exports now **level the sound** of the whole film: for the web (-16 LUFS) by default, for broadcast (-23 LUFS), or as mixed. The film never gets so loud that it clips.
