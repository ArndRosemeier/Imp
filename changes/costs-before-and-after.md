# See what things cost, before and after

- Every button that spends money now shows the expected price on it: portraits, voices, key frames, clips, dialogue, sounds, music, location pictures, recorded lines and transcripts. For example "Generate clip ≈ $0.20".
- Clip prices come from the video model's own price list, by length, size and sound. Other prices come from what the same model cost you before; until a model has been used once, a dashed price shows a rough typical one.
- An agent's approval card shows the price of what it asks to do; giving an agent a task shows its budget limit.
- A running total in the top bar shows what the open project has cost. Open it for today, all projects, a breakdown by kind and the latest calls.
- Agents can ask what anything would cost before doing it, and what has been spent so far.
