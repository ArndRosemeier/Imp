# See clips that are being made

- A shot whose clip is being made now shows a "Rendering" badge with a spinner, and the scene tabs in Shots show how many clips are rendering in each scene.
- Shots also lists clips rendering in other scenes, so one tap takes you to them even when another scene is open.
- On the Edit timeline, a clip that is still being made shows as a small pin where it will join the cut, and the top of Edit says how many are rendering.
- Imp now checks on every clip being made from any stage, including clips an agent started, so they arrive without opening their scene in Shots.
- A clip that is not done within an hour, or that the video service lost, now ends as failed with a Try again button instead of waiting for ever.
- Agents now copy shot and scene ids exactly from what Imp tells them, instead of quoting ids that do not exist.
