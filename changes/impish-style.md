# A little more imp

- Imp now has a name to live up to: Imp Makes Pictures. Above the Projects heading it tries on another meaning each visit; press it to see the next one.
- The imp's horns twitch and its tail flicks when you point at the logo, and once when Imp opens.
- Tips come from the imp and carry its mark. Empty stages show their icon under a pair of small horns, with a short aside.
- While an agent works, a little tail chases itself where it used to say "Working".
- Ask Imp talks like an imp: friendly, quick and to the point, with a light touch of wit now and then, never about prices or problems.
