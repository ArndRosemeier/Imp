# A read-through before the shots

- Script has **notes** under each scene: a problem, a to-do or a note, by you or by the crew. Mark them done, open them again or remove them.
- The **Critic** now reads the script before any picture is made and leaves its notes there, where a change still costs nothing. The Screenwriter works through the open notes when it revises a scene.
- The coordinator has the script read through before the storyboard when a Critic is in the crew, and otherwise offers once to hire one.
- The stages now run in the order a film is made: Development, Casting, Design, Script, Shots, Edit, Sound. The guided first film casts before it writes.
