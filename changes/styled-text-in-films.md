# Styled words and easier placing for text in films

- Words in titles, subtitles, lower thirds, captions and credits can now be styled on their own: bold, italic, underlined, coloured, smaller or bigger. Select words and tap a style button, or press Ctrl+B, I or U. Agents write the same marks (`*italic*`, `**bold**`, `{#ffd166|colour}`).
- Drag a selected text on the picture to place it, or nudge it with the arrow keys. It snaps to the centre and to the safe area, and turns red when it is close enough to the edge for some screens to cut it off.
- A selected text is shown on the picture even when the playhead is outside its time, so it can always be seen and placed.
- Centred texts and subtitles now break into even lines instead of leaving one word alone on the second line.
- Subtitles that are too fast to read are flagged on the timeline and in the text panel.
- End credits are set like real credits: roles and headings small, names large.
- Subtitle files (.srt and .vtt) keep italic, bold, underline and colour.
- Fixed: the preview drew texts in fallback fonts instead of Imp's own, so a text could look different on the Edit screen than in the exported film.
- Fixed: bold serif text looked different in the browser and in films exported by the local server.
