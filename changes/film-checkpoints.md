# The crew stops for you at checkpoints

- The crew now stops at three checkpoints so you can look before anything expensive is made: the treatment, the storyboard of each scene and the first cut of each scene. Press **Sign off** in Development, above the shots and in the Edit bar.
- Casting, design and the scenes wait for the treatment, and the clips of a scene wait for its storyboard, so no clip money is spent on a scene you have not seen as frames.
- New in Development: **How much the crew does on its own**. Stop at each checkpoint (the default), only at the storyboard, or let it make everything.
- Creating a project no longer starts casting, writing and shots by itself. The coordinator asks what you want first.
- Clips bring their own sound, so the crew no longer makes sound effects and music for every scene. It adds sound when you ask, or where a first cut lacks it. Sound now comes after Edit.
