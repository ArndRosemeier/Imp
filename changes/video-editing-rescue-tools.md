# Speed, freeze frames, reverse and punch-in

- Select a shot on the Edit stage to find "Speed, freeze and zoom". Play it at ¼× to 4×, or backwards.
- Freeze a shot: hold its last frame for a few seconds, or press "Freeze at playhead" to stop the picture at a moment and then let it play on.
- Punch in: zoom into part of the frame to turn a wide shot into a closer one or hide a flaw at the edge. Tap the little frame to choose where it looks, and tick "Move over the shot" for a slow push in or pull out.
- The preview and the exported film show exactly the same thing.
- A slowed, sped up or reversed shot plays without its own sound, as in a real edit. Lay sound for it on the audio lane.
- "Play as filmed" puts a shot back the way it was made.
- The Film Editor can use all of these tools too.
