# A visual style that keeps the film one film

- Development has a **Visual style** section, the film's look bible: palette, light, camera and era in a few words, and a **look picture** you paint or drop in from the library.
- Every portrait, place picture and key frame is now drawn in that style, and gets the chosen look picture as a reference where the image model takes one. Hundreds of shots keep one look.
- The tone is now just genre and mood. Films without a visual style keep drawing in their tone, as before.
- The Production Designer sets the look first, before any portrait or key frame is drawn.
