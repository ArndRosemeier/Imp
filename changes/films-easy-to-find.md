# Find the films your crew makes

- When an agent finishes a film, a notice pops up with **Watch** and **Save as…**, and its reply in the chat carries a card to play and save the film right there.
- The Library has a new **Films** tab with every film of the project, to play, save or delete.
- Every film now shows when it was made, who made it and how long it runs, and two renders of the same cut get different names (film, film-2, …).
- **Save as…** asks where to put the MP4 instead of a plain download.
- Agents only call a film done once the file really exists; an export that writes nothing now fails with a clear message.
