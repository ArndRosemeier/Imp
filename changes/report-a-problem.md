# Report a problem, and the guide on the web

- The Help panel has a new Report a problem tab. Say what happened, and Imp writes a report with its version, where it runs, your screen and the last few commands with their errors. It opens as a filled-in GitHub issue for you to check and submit, or you can copy it. Your key, prompts and story never go in.
- Ask Imp and the coordinator can write the report for you.
- The guide is now also a website, published beside the app. "The guide on the web" at the bottom of the Help panel opens it.
- Failed commands in the activity log now say why they failed, not only that they did.
