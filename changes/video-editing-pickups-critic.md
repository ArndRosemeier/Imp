# Pickup shots and a critic

- Missing a shot in a scene? Select a piece in the Edit screen and choose "Ask for a shot after this". Say what the camera shows and what the cut needs it for.
- The new shot goes into the cut right away as a striped slate of the length you asked for, so you can time the scene around it. It also appears on the Shots screen marked "Pickup", with the reason.
- When its clip is made, the clip takes the slate's place by itself.
- The Film Editor can ask for pickups too, and the Clip Designer finds the open ones to make.
- New crew role: the Critic. It watches a scene or the whole film and leaves notes, to-dos and problems as markers on the timeline. It never changes the film.
