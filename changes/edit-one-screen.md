# The Edit stage fits one screen

- The picture, the tools and the timeline now fit on one screen, on a tablet as well as a short desktop window. The timeline stays in view at the bottom, and the picture shrinks to fit the room that is left.
- Drag the bar above the tools to give the picture or the timeline more room. Double-click it to go back to the default.
- Export and the film's Look open from buttons at the top. Help with the keys and gestures is under the question mark.
- Title cards, text, sounds and shots that are not in the film yet are all under Add. A number on Add shows how many shots are waiting.
- The film strip is one slim row. When the stage is narrow, for example on a tablet with the crew open, the details of the selected piece slide over the picture and close with ×.
- Every editing button is now at least a fingertip in size, including zoom, which also has new − and + buttons.
