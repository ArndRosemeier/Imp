# Shots that flow into each other

- Each shot now says how it comes after the one before: a new angle or place, **carries on the same take**, or a **cut on action**, with a note on why the cut is there.
- A shot that carries on the same take starts on the last frame of the clip before, so the action flows across the cut. It waits until that clip is ready.
- A cut on action gets a key frame painted to match where the shot before ends, the same instant from a new angle.
- New **end frames**: paint the picture a clip should land on, and models that take one end the clip on it.
- The Film Editor never trims a join that carries on frame to frame, and the edit list flags a shot made from an older clip of the one before.
