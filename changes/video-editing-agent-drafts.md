# The Film Editor proposes, you decide

- The Film Editor's edits now arrive as proposed changes, like tracked changes: switch between Cut and Proposal above the timeline, play the proposal, and keep or reject each change, or all at once. Keeping is one step Undo takes back.
- Mark a stretch of a scene with In and Out (keys I and O) and your next message to the crew is about just that stretch.
- The Film Editor can now see the cut: it reads each scene as an edit list with every spoken line and its timing, and looks at stills from the clips.
