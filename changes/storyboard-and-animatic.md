# Storyboard and animatic

- Shots shows each scene as a **storyboard**: every shot as its key frame, in order, with its length, how it joins and its line. Tap a frame to jump to the shot.
- Before a shot has its clip, its key frame plays in the Edit preview as a still, for as long as the shot should hold, with its line under it. A planned scene plays as an **animatic**, so you can judge its timing before any clip is paid for.
- Exporting a scene that only has stills gives a film of the animatic, with the lines as captions.
