# Each crew role works in its own area

- Every role now changes only its own work. The Screenwriter writes the development and the scenes, and reads the cast's details, but can no longer make or change characters; the Casting Director cannot write scenes, and so on for every role.
- A crew member that finds something missing, such as a character a scene needs, stops and says what is missing instead of making it up. The chat shows "Something is missing", and Continue picks up once it is there.
- Each role says what it needs before it starts: the Screenwriter a cast before it writes scenes, the Casting Director and the Production Designer the development, the Film Editor clips. The coordinator checks this before it hands out work and has the missing piece made first. A film with no characters can go without a cast.
- The coordinator coordinates: it sets things up and hands out the work instead of doing the crew's work itself.
- The coordinator and the Screenwriter work with models that read only text. They are never shown pictures, clips or sound.
- What a role must see decides which models fit it. Roles that judge pictures need a model that sees them. Hiring one with a model that doesn't is refused, and only models that fit are offered.
- Your own roles hold all of this too: what they read, what they may change, what they must see, web search, and what they need before they start. **Change** on a custom role opens it again.
