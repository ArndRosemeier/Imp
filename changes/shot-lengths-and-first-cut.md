# Shots of their own length, and a first cut that trims them

- Every shot now says how long it holds in the cut, and why: a glance a second or two, a line as long as it takes to say, a walk as long as the walk. The Clip Designer plans varied lengths instead of making every shot the same.
- Clips are made a little longer than the shot holds, so the edit can cut in late and out early, at the best moment.
- A spoken line on screen now counts toward the length of its clip, so lines are no longer squeezed.
- New on the Edit stage: **Trim to planned length** cuts every shot down to its planned length in one go, or just the selected one. The Film Editor makes a first cut this way and then moves each cut to a movement, a look or the end of a line, and asks first when it is not sure you want one.
