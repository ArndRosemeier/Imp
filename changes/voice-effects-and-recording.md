# Voice effects and microphone recording

- Voices can now be changed in the audio editor: distortion, pitch shift, ring modulation and bit crush, each with its own settings.
- One tap gives a ready-made voice: telephone, radio, robot, megaphone, monster or chipmunk.
- A record button in the audio editor records from your microphone at the playhead, with a live level meter. Press R to start.
- Agents can apply voices and keep recordings through the same commands.
