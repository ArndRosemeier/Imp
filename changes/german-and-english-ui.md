# Imp speaks German

- Choose the language of Imp under Settings, or on the first screen when you set Imp up: English or German. English stays the default.
- Everything switches at once: every stage, the library, the crew panel, tips, the guide, Ask Imp and What's new. Numbers, prices and dates follow the language too.
- Agents answer you in the language you chose. Your films keep the language they are written in, unless you ask for another.
- Agents can switch the language for you ("switch Imp to German"), and Save all keeps the choice.
