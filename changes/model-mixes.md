**Model mixes.** Keep every model you use (agents, video, voices, pictures, sound effects, music, transcripts) under a name, such as a cheap mix for drafts and a better one for the final cut, and switch the whole set at once. Each stage now shows its models as one small chip: tap it to switch mixes, change a single model, or save the models as a mix. The library has a new Mixes tab to use, rename, update and delete them, and agents can do all of this too. Mixes belong to your workspace, so they work in every project.
