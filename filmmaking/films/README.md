## The Leaf

Folder `leaf-journey`. A maple leaf falls from a tree and travels in one
unbroken take through a meadow, over a lake with koi swimming in the air,
through a space that is both deep sea and night sky, out of a teacup and onto
an open book.

- **Purpose:** the first test. Can a film of any length be made as one take
  with real continuity?
- **Made of:** 10 stills (a reference picture of the leaf, six anchors and
  repaints), 5 clips of 8 to 10 seconds (one filmed twice), 1 score.
- **Cost:** $3.75 video, $0.10 stills, $0.08 music.
- **Result:** the leaf stays the same leaf, the joins are exact. Expected
  weakness: pauses at the four joins.
- **Arnd's verdict:** not yet given.
- **Final file:** `Director/movies/leaf-journey/cut/the-leaf_v1.mp4` on Drive.

## The Kobold Cave

Folder `kobold-cave`. Brann the fighter and Fenn the druid, old friends who
bicker, take a job to clear a kobold cave: tavern, cave mouth, tunnel, a
tripwire, a short fight, the loot.

- **Purpose:** the second test. Cuts, reusable characters, spoken dialogue,
  and a workflow that can be repeated.
- **Made of:** 2 characters and a creature for the library, 4 location
  masters, 19 keyframes (2 repainted), 19 shots (3 filmed twice), 2 music
  beds, 18 cuts including J-cuts, an L-cut and a graphic match cut.
- **Cost:** $7.65 video, $0.30 stills, $0.08 music, the rest tests. $9.12
  with the voice tests made after delivery.
- **Result:** every scripted line is spoken, characters and places hold
  across the cuts.
- **Arnd's verdict:** voice coherence "pretty good". Found the dart in the
  last shot on the wrong side of Brann, and left it unfixed as a lesson.
- **Final file:** `Director/movies/kobold-cave/cut/the-kobold-cave_v1.mp4` on
  Drive.
